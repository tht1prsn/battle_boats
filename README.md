## BattleBoats

A game where 2 players connect via firebase to play a game of BattleShips

## Who this app is for

This app is for anyone who wants to play battleship online with friends

## What it does

This app uses firebase to connect two users to play a game. One player creates a lobby and the second player joins the lobby via a join code. The two users play battleship by placing their own boats, and marking the opponents board to look for their boats. When one player marks all of the opponents' boats, that player wins.
